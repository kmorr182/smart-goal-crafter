What is a SMART goal?

A SMART goal turns a vague idea • "get healthier," "learn a language" • into something specific enough to actually act on. SMART stands for:

Specific
A clear, concrete statement of what you'll do.
Measurable
A number or finish line you can check yourself against.
Achievable
Realistic given your actual time, resources, and skills.
Relevant
Tied to something that actually matters to you.
Time-bound
A real date, not "eventually."

Frequently asked questions

What does SMART stand for?

Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound • a goal-setting framework that turns a vague idea into something concrete enough to act on.

Is SMART Goal Crafter free to use?

Yes. There's no account, no sign-up, and no cost • just describe your idea and build out your goal.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start building a goal immediately, with no sign-up required.

Can I save or share the goal I create?

Yes. Once your goal is complete, you can save it, and share it by email, on X, or by copying the text.

What if I'm not confident my goal is realistic?

The Achievable step asks you to rate your confidence from 1 to 10, and nudges you if it looks like too big a stretch, or too easy, so you can recalibrate before committing.
